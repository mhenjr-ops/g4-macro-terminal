Section 01

Global Situation & Capital Flow Narrative

Desk View

Three forces are currently setting the direction of cross-border capital: the front-end yield differential between the G4 central banks, a geopolitical risk premium that is being expressed through energy and the haven ladder rather than through classic risk-off, and crude anchored in the $80–$90 band, which sits directly on the terms-of-trade pivot for every energy importer in the complex. Expand each driver for the flow mechanics.

Net Flow Conclusion

Capital is being paid to stay long carry and long dollars, but it is being paid less each month as the differential compresses — while the tail risk of a disorderly carry unwind grows with the crowd. The desk's posture is therefore participate with the differential, size for the unwind: full-size trades only where trend location, momentum and carry all agree, and half-size or flat everywhere else.
